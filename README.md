# Ala Carta 

Ala Carta is a SaaS backend platform for digital menus designed for cafés and restaurants.

## Problem

Many cafés still use static menus, PDFs, or menus that are difficult to update, navigate, or personalize.

Ala Carta solves problems such as:

- Outdated prices
- Products shown as available when they're sold out
- Difficult QR menu navigation
- Poor menu organization
- Lack of brand identity in digital menus

## Main Features (MVP)

### Business Owners
- User registration and login
- Business creation
- Branch management
- Product categories
- Product management
- Product availability
- Menu branding customization

### Customers
- Mobile-friendly digital menu
- Intuitive category navigation
- Fast access via QR
